What sets it apart · 1

Onboarding? Schraubi takes care of it.

No training day, no manual, nobody has to sit next to new staff: Mendura ships with its own trainer. Schraubi, the gear mascot, guides every person through the app step by step, on real workflows instead of slides.

  • 9 chapters, split by role: workers learn reporting, maintaining and completing. Planners and administrators get their own chapters.
  • Real exercises: report a fault, perform a maintenance, close an order. Schraubi highlights the right spot on screen.
  • With knowledge checks: progress and completions are tracked per person, so the foreman sees who is ready.
What sets it apart · 2

Maintenance that people enjoy

The best app is useless if the team does not embrace it. So Mendura rewards finished work with points and turns a tedious duty into a bit of friendly competition.

  • Points for real work: inspections, maintenance, repairs and reported faults all add to your personal score.
  • A leaderboard for the team: medals, ranks and a weekly score. Staying on it pays off.
  • Visibility for management: the foreman sees in black and white who documents reliably. The team stays motivated while management keeps the overview.
Why Mendura

Born on the shop floor, not on a drawing board

Certified Industrial Foreman ·
Trainer & IHK examiner

I am a certified industrial foreman (Industriemeister Metall) and work as a full-time trainer: I teach skilled workers pneumatics, PLC technology, manual and machine processing of metals, and plastics technology. Alongside that I handle a lot of organisational work, create technical drawings in CAD and serve as an IHK examiner, among others for future industrial foremen.

Our facility ran the way many workshops still do: breakdown maintenance. A machine got attention only once it had actually failed. And of course it failed at the worst possible moment, right before examination days when every machine is needed. Then it was repairing under stress and frantically ordering spare parts.

The solution has been known for decades: preventive maintenance with a proper plan. It extends machine life and avoids exactly those firefighting missions. What was missing was a tool for it that does not require a corporate IT project.

Mendura started small: as a 3D-printing hobbyist I first just wanted a maintenance app for my own printer. That grew into the realisation that exactly this kind of tool helps businesses that still run on paper or Excel.

So Mendura is built the way I would use it myself: fully offline, data stays on site, no account, no cloud. And simple enough that everyone on the team can work with it without a training day. Schraubi handles the onboarding, right inside the app.

Alexander UshakovFounder, Mendura Digital
