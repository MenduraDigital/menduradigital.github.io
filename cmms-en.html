What sets Mendura apart · 1

Onboarding? Schraubi takes care of it.

No training day, no manual, nobody has to sit next to the new colleague: Mendura brings its own trainer. Schraubi, the little gear mascot, guides every person through the app step by step, using real workflows instead of slides.

  • 9 chapters, separated by role: technicians learn reporting, servicing and completing work. Planners and administrators get their own chapters.
  • Hands-on exercises: report a fault, carry out a service, close a work order. Schraubi highlights exactly where to tap.
  • With comprehension checks: progress and completions are recorded per person, so the supervisor can see who is up to speed.
What sets Mendura apart · 2

Maintenance people enjoy

Even the best app is useless if the team does not embrace it. That is why Mendura rewards completed work with points and turns a chore into a friendly competition.

  • Points for real work: inspections, services, repairs and reported faults all count towards your score.
  • A leaderboard for the team: medals, ranks and a weekly score. Consistent work is rewarded.
  • Visibility for management: the team overview shows clearly what has been documented and completed. The team stays motivated while management retains a clear overview.

Frequently asked

Answered briefly

Does Mendura really work without an internet connection?
Yes. Every function runs offline on the device. Multiple devices sync directly over your local Wi-Fi, signed and encrypted. There is no cloud and there are no servers on our side.
What does Mendura CMMS cost?
€19.99 per month or €199 per year, per company. You can try everything free of charge for 30 days first.
How many devices and employees can I use?
As many as you like. One licence covers the whole company, all devices included. There is no per-seat pricing.
Can I manage vehicles such as forklifts?
Yes. Vehicles are kept with licence plate and VIN, and maintenance plans can run by time, operating hours or mileage.
Where is my data stored?
Exclusively on your own devices. There is no account with us and no transfer to third parties. You create backups yourself and can encrypt them with AES-256.
How much effort is it to roll out across the team?
Onboarding is handled by the built-in Schraubi training: 9 chapters with hands-on exercises, separated by role, with progress tracked per person. A separate training day is usually not necessary.
Which businesses is Mendura made for?
Small and medium-sized businesses: machining, metalwork, plastics, workshops, training centres, facility management and vehicle fleets. Anywhere paper or spreadsheets still run maintenance today.
