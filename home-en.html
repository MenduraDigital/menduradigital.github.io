Mendura Home

There is always a project going on at my place: the 3D printer is running, the tools are out, and sooner or later all of it needs maintenance. Mendura started as a maintenance plan for my own printer. Mendura Home turns that into a lean planner for everything at home that runs, cuts, prints and drives.
